# VOICE LINES — Night in Brainrot Valley

> Story dialogue: screenplay.md is the authoritative script. If a line here differs, use the screenplay version. This file mainly defines voice settings, intro chants and extra lobby/daytime lines.

How voices work in the game:
- Default: Roblox built-in text-to-speech (`AudioTextToSpeech`, max 300 characters per line). Each character has its own Pitch and Speed so they sound different.
- Optional upgrade: record the same lines with an external TTS (deep, dramatic, Italian-accented narrator voice — the same *style* as the brainrot memes), export .ogg, upload to Roblox, and paste the SoundId into the line's `soundId` field. If `soundId` is set, it plays instead of TTS.
- Every line is ALSO shown as a subtitle in the dialogue box.

Style rule for brainrot characters: every character introduces themselves with their name chanted rhythmically, then speaks in short dramatic sentences with Italian-sounding words mixed in.

## Story lines

### Chapter 1 — Arrival
- Narrator: "Brainrot Valley. A quiet village. Nobody leaves after dark… and nobody remembers why."
- Giallino: "Ciao, new friends! I'm Giallino, your little light! I always tell the truth! I'll keep you safe. Just do what I say, okay?"
- Giallino: "Wood is to the north. Seventeen blocks. I counted them for you!"
- Chimpanzini Bananini: "You want lantern? Lantern is good. Night is NOT good."
- Giallino: "Get inside before the sun goes down. That's rule number one!"

### Chapter 2 — First Night
- Narrator: "And then… the knocking began."
- Tung Tung Tung Sahur (behind door, muffled): "Tung… tung… tung…"
- Giallino: "Don't open it. Don't open it. Don't. Open. It."
- (Morning) Tralalero Tralala: "Ballerina is GONE! Her door is broken… from the inside! Tralala, what does it mean?!"
- Giallino: "It was Tung Tung Tung. I saw everything. Trust me. You trust me, right?"

### Chapter 3 — Investigation
- Cappuccino Assassino: "Footprints. Too round for feet. Think about it."
- Bombardiro Crocodilo: "From the sky I saw a light. Orange. Floating. Going into Ballerina's house!"
- Brr Brr Patapim: "Who smiles in the dark but has no face? Answer me… patapim."
- Lirili's clock (note read by narrator): "If you hear this, I was wrong. The light I found cannot hear the word no."
- Falsino: "That clue is fake! Someone put it there! Here, I found the REAL one for you."
- Vote prompt (Narrator): "Who knocks at night? Choose carefully. Someone will be locked away."

### Chapter 4 — Second Night
- Giallino (corrupting): "Why did you say no, [DisplayName]? I s-saw you. In the inn. Under the bed. Twice."
- Negatino: "Your lantern is so small. The dark is so big. Why do you even try?"
- Tung Tung Tung Sahur (shouting through door): "WAKE UP! It is not me! RUN to the forest before the sun comes back WRONG!"

### Chapter 5 — The Mineshaft
- Lirili Larila (frozen, slow): "You… found me. Tic… tac… The gears. Three gears. Bring time back."

### Chapter 6 — Finale
- Giallino Totale (all voices layered): "I was the light. I was the truth. Now I am everything you were afraid of."
- Tung Tung Tung Sahur: "Go! I hold the door. Tung. Tung. TUNG!"

- Endless Night: Giallino — "Welcome back, friends! I'm Giallino, your little light…"
